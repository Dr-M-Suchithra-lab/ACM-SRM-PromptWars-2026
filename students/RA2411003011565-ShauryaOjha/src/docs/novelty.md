# What Makes FairShift Different

Most entries for this brief will build a dashboard with attendance, a complaint form and a generic chatbot. FairShift's choices start from who the worker is: low literacy, a regional language, a basic phone, and a real fear of speaking up.

| #   | What we did                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Why it matters                                                                                                                                                                                        | Where it lives                                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **The honest muster roll.** The UI borrows the worker's own paperwork: register grid, punch-clock times, and a **rubber stamp** on every real confirmation ("CHECKED IN 08:02", "PAID", "RESOLVED")                                                                                                                                                                            | A low-literacy user recognises a stamp instantly. It is a confirmation they already trust, not a toast that disappears                                                                                | `src/components/ui/Stamp.tsx`, `docs/design-system.md`   |
| 2   | **Rules are code; AI only explains.** Overtime (OSH Code 2020: 8 h/day, 48 h/week, 2×), PF/ESI, fatigue limits, hazard deadlines and quit-risk are pure, tested functions                                                                                                                                                                                                      | A payslip a worker can trust can't come from a model that guesses. Gemini rephrases the result in plain Hindi or Tamil, and every AI feature has a rule-based fallback                                | `src/server/domain/*`, `src/server/ai/*`                 |
| 3   | **Voice-first in the worker's language.** Speak a hazard report in Hindi or Tamil, hear the payslip read aloud, and use icons with one word on every tile                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Research on low-literacy users in India shows text-only apps fail first-time users, while voice and pictures work                                                                                     | `/w/*`, `src/i18n/*`                                     |
| 4   | **Anonymity by design, not by promise.** Feedback is stored with **no worker id**. Team results stay hidden until **3 people** answer, and names and phone numbers are scrubbed from comments, including Hindi and Tamil names                                                                                                                                                 | Fear of identification is the brief's second pain point. A promise of anonymity isn't enough; the data simply never holds the link                                                                    | `src/server/domain/anonymity.ts`                         |
| 5   | **Hazards escalate themselves.** Each severity has a deadline (critical 1 h … low 72 h). A missed deadline is computed **on read**, so it escalates without any cron job or background worker                                                                                                                                                                                  | Escalation can't be forgotten, switched off or delayed by infrastructure                                                                                                                              | `src/server/domain/hazardSla.ts`                         |
| 6   | **Explainable quit-risk.** A 0–100 score built from overtime load, fatigue flags, attendance drop, unresolved complaints and team sentiment, always shown with its **top 3 reasons** and real numbers                                                                                                                                                                          | A supervisor can act on "66 h last week, 7 fatigue flags, attendance down 14%". A bare score gives them nothing to act on                                                                             | `src/server/domain/quitRisk.ts`, `workforceSignals.ts`   |
| 7   | **Check-in with nothing to install.** A rotating 6-digit QR at the gate (new code every 60 s) is scanned with the phone's own camera, with a GPS geofence as the alternative. A signed-out scan survives login                                                                                                                                                                 | It works on any phone, and a screenshot of yesterday's QR is useless                                                                                                                                  | `src/server/domain/qrCode.ts`, `geofence.ts`, `proxy.ts` |
| 8   | **One model, many sectors.** Construction and college campus run live from one data model. A regional HR lead switches between them; a site admin only sees their own                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The brief requires 2+ industries. Hospitals and factories need only seed data                                                                                                                         | `src/server/services/sectors.ts`                         |
| 9   | **Keeps working when parts fail.** If Gemini is slow it falls back to rule-based text. If MongoDB is unreachable, the app keeps serving from a seeded store instead of erroring                                                                                                                                                                                                | A demo, or a site office with bad internet, never gets a blank screen                                                                                                                                 | `src/server/repos/index.ts`                              |
| 10  | **Built for a 2 GB phone in sunlight.** No animation library on worker routes, fonts loaded only for the chosen language, a light high-contrast theme, and 48 px+ targets. axe found **0 issues** on every worker screen                                                                                                                                                       | Inclusivity is measured, not claimed                                                                                                                                                                  | `docs/design-system.md` §6–8                             |
| 11  | **An assistant that cannot invent a number or read someone else's data.** Urima answers in Hindi, Tamil or English by calling read-only tools that take **no parameters** and are bound on the server to the signed-in worker (or the supervisor's permitted sector). Every answer links to the records it used, and without AI the same tools answer through a keyword router | Most chatbots put the model in charge. Here the rule engines stay in charge: prompt injection has nothing to widen, and the feature still works with no API key, on a rate limit, or offline-adjacent | `src/server/assistant/*`                                 |

**In one line:** FairShift makes the wage and safety record that workers already keep on paper honest, readable and spoken, and it gives employers warnings early enough to act.
