Privacy policy

Shiftseal (web app and Android app) · Last updated 1 November 2026

Shiftseal keeps a tamper-evident record of working time, pay, leave and safety reports for the people who work at a workplace and for the employer who runs it. This page says what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

What we collect, and why

Data Why
Phone number or Google account email, and your name To sign you in and show your name to your own workplace.
Your location, only at the moment you check in or out To show the check happened at the workplace. Shiftseal never tracks location in the background.
Whether a fake-location app is active, and a Google Play Integrity verdict To stop forged check-ins (Android app only).
A device key created on your phone To sign each check so nobody can change it later.
Shift times, pay, leave, overtime claims, hazard reports and photos you take This is the work record itself, shared with your workplace.
Voice notes Turned into text by Google's Gemini model and then discarded. The audio is never stored.
Anonymous feedback Names and places are removed before it is saved; your words are not kept, only the cleaned summary, and your employer sees it only in groups of five or more.
Student status and date of birth, if you add them To apply the hour limits for students and young workers. Your employer sees a badge, never your birth date.
Harassment complaints Seen only by the complaints committee you send them to.

Camera access is used to scan the gate QR code and to photograph hazards. The microphone is used only while you hold the talk button. Shiftseal has no advertising, no analytics trackers and does not sell or rent any data.

Who else handles it

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Firestore) stores and serves the records.
  • Firebase Authentication handles sign-in.
  • Gemini on Vertex AI reads text and voice notes you choose to send, to understand them. Google does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google Play Integrity checks that the Android app is genuine.

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

How long it is kept

Work records are kept while your workplace uses Shiftseal, because labour law requires employers to keep time and wage records (for example, three years in India and the United States). Sign-in data and your profile are kept until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete my account. Your name, phone number, email, student status, date of birth and device keys are erased straight away, and you leave every workplace. Shift records that were already sealed stay with the employer under "Former member", without your name or number, because the law requires wage records to be kept and the sealed chain cannot be edited.

If you cannot open the app, or you own a workplace that still has people in it, email sushilraj0177@gmail.com from any address with the phone number or email you signed in with. We complete requests within 30 days.

Children

Shiftseal is for working people and their employers. It is not directed at children under 13. Where the law allows young people to work (for example, 14 to 17 in some countries), Shiftseal applies the extra hour and night-work limits for them.
