How it works

  1. Check in

    Scan the QR code at your site or tap "I am at work". It works with no signal too.

  2. See your pay

    Your payslip explains hours, overtime and deductions in plain words, and tells you if you were underpaid.

  3. Speak up safely

    Report a hazard or a complaint with icons, your voice or a photo. You can keep your name out of it.
