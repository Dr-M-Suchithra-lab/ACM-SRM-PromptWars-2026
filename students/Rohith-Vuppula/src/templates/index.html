6 Core Working Features (UN SDG 8 Aligned)

Every feature is designed specifically for deskless frontline workers and supervisors to eliminate wage theft and workplace safety hazards.

1. Shift Attendance & Overtime Tracker

1-Click QR check-in simulates on-site scanning. Distinguishes standard 8.0-hour duty from extra duty, automatically computing statutory 1.5× overtime pay.

⚡ Fatigue Guardrail: Exceeding 48 hours weekly triggers warning badges on both worker slips and admin rosters.

2. Transparent Multilingual Payslip

Clear mathematical wage formula eliminating pay disputes before pay day. Displays gross pay, statutory 5% PF/ESI deductions, and net take-home pay.

🌐 4 Languages: Instant localized translations in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Tamil (தமிழ்), and Telugu (తెలుగు).

3. Zero-Knowledge Anonymous Reporting

Frontline workers can report hazardous conditions (faulty scaffolding, exposed wires) or wage discrepancies without fear of termination or retaliation.

🔒 Cryptographic Tickets: Worker identity and IP are stripped. Status tracked solely via unique FS-XXXXX codes.

4. Complaint & Evidence Organiser

Submit structured complaints for unpaid overtime, withheld salary, or harassment with supporting evidence (salary slips, attendance registers, messages).

📂 Evidence Vault: Categorizes supporting documentation for labour authorities and legal aid counsel.

5. Legal Aid & Authority Referral

Direct connection to District Labour Commissioners, NALSA free legal aid, EPFO, ESIC, and specialised worker welfare boards across India.

🏛️ Official Channels: Verified government portals and helpline access without middleman exploitation.

6. Exploitation Prevention Radar

Stops exploitation before it occurs: monitors excessive shift hours (>48h/wk), tracks unresolved complaints, and produces real-time compliance grades.

📊 Proactive Audits: AI-driven compliance recommendations keep organisations accountable.
