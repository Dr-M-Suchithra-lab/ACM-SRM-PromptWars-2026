How it works

Three things every worker can do in under a minute

Check in, no QR needed

The supervisor's screen shows a code that changes every 5 minutes. Workers type it on a big number pad. No phone? The supervisor marks them present.

See and hear your pay

Every hour is counted. Overtime and night allowance follow the industry's rules, and the payslip is read aloud in the worker's language.

Speak up safely

Hold a button and describe the problem in any language. AI turns it into a clear report for the manager. Unanswered critical issues escalate automatically.

Works across industries

One platform, different rules per industry

Each industry is a small settings file. Adding a factory or a campus is a new file, not new code.
